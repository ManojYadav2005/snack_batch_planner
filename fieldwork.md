# Fieldwork

**Location:** Ramesh Patel's Chai & Snack Stall — Hostel-A gate, Engineering College Campus.

**Fieldwork period:** 8 Nov – 11 Nov 2026 (four days).  
**Observation hours:** 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM each day (Ramesh fries snacks in two shifts: morning batch at 10 AM and post-lunch batch at 1 PM; we stopped counting at 4 PM when the second batch was largely sold or discarded).

## Conversation with the Stall Owner

- **Name:** Ramesh Patel  
- **Role:** Sole owner and cook at the snack stall, Hostel-A gate. Has run this stall for 11 years.

**Three things Ramesh said:**
1. "Roz subah 30-30-20 bana leta hoon — samosa, puff, vada. Kabhi sab bik jaata hai, kabhi adha phek deta hoon." *(Every morning I make 30 samosas, 30 puffs, 20 vada. Sometimes everything sells, sometimes I throw half away.)*
2. "Puff sabse zyada waste hoti hai — do ghante baad soft ho jaati hai, koi nahi leta." *(Bread puffs waste the most — after two hours they go soft and nobody takes them.)*
3. "Exam ke din samosa pehle khatam hota hai, students zyada lete hain. Us din mujhe pata nahi hota kitna banana hai." *(On exam days samosas finish first, students buy more. On those days I don't know how many to make.)*

**One thing that surprised me:**  
Ramesh told me he has tried reducing the vada batch to 10 pieces on slow days, but hostel staff (not students) specifically come for vada between 3–4 PM, and if he has fewer than 15 he runs out before they arrive. He now keeps a mental note of "hostel warden ki timing" (the warden's timing) — an entirely separate demand segment from the student rush that he manages entirely by memory and that no inventory tool would capture without being told explicitly.

## Raw Data — Collection Note

Data was recorded in a pocket notebook at the stall. For each hourly slot, I noted:
- **item**: type of snack (samosa / puff / vada)
- **batch_fried**: number of pieces put out at the start of the slot (new pieces placed on the tray)
- **sold**: number of pieces sold during that hour (counted by watching Ramesh hand pieces to customers and pack them)
- **thrown**: number of pieces discarded at the end of that slot as unsellable (too cold/soft)
- **carry_over**: leftover pieces still on the tray (edible but unsold, carried to the next hour)

Counts were confirmed with Ramesh at the end of each hour. Data was transferred to `data.csv` the same evening.

The raw data is in [data.csv](data.csv).
